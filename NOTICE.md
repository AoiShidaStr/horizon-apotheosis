Clean-room reimplementation. The Rust code in `native/apotheosis/api/`, the
data in `content/`, and the placeholder textures generated by
`tools/gen_content.py` are original to this port and MIT licensed.

## Upstream

Apotheosis — https://github.com/Shadows-of-Fire/Apotheosis — by Shadows_of_Fire,
MIT licensed. Its gems, tiers, affixes, reforging and enchanting were studied
through public documentation and wiki pages for behaviour only. **No Apotheosis
source code, data file, model or texture is included in this folder.**
